# CLUTRL Cross-Platform Brand Kit

CLUTRL is pronounced **Clue Trail**. This kit implements the approved split-word
identity with an angular slash and scan-frame app symbol.

- `01_Master_Logos/SVG/CLUTRL_Primary_Dark.svg` — primary logo on dark backgrounds

## File guidance

- SVG/PDF: preferred for printers, signage, screen printing, and scalable digital use.
- PNG: transparent raster artwork and channel-sized exports.
- iOS app icons are opaque. Android includes an adaptive foreground and background color.
- Apparel vectors use flat colors and no effects. Embroidery still requires decorator digitizing and a sew-out.

## Color priority

Trail Black + Beacon Lime is the signature pairing. Field Cream is the preferred
warm neutral. Rally Coral is for live urgency and competition. Signal Cyan is for
navigation and progress. Use one bright accent at a time in most executions.

## Domains

- `CLUTRL.com`: organizers, partners, platform, pricing, and management.
- `CLUTRL.fun`: participants, QR entry, live hunts, rewards, and event links.
