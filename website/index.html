The loop

Four beats. Every discovery.

The same sequence repeats at every target, whether you're chasing one clue or all ten — read, find, prove it, get rewarded.

01

Join the hunt

Enter a hunt code or scan the event's start QR. Guests can jump in with just a name — no account wall.

02

Read the clue

Text, photo, video, or a geolocated AR moment — every clue points to one real, physical target.

03

Scan to confirm

Find the printed marker and scan it. Verification happens on the server — no guessing, no faking it.

04

Claim your reward

Finish the trail and unlock a single-use reward code, issued only to teams who actually completed it.
